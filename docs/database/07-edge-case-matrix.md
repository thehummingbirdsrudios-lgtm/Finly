# 7. Database edge-case matrix

Add-on 11 item 28 and §30 item 14: every scenario simulated against the schema before implementation. Each row says
which rows are written, what the **database itself** guarantees, what the engine/API guarantees, and the result.
"DB test" names the test in `backend/tests/db/` that proves the database's part. Rows needing two concurrent
connections (#24) run only against PostgreSQL 17 in CI; PGlite has one connection.

Amounts are the owner's examples. Notation: `[E]` = entity E's journal.

| # | Scenario | Rows written | Database guarantees | Engine / API guarantees | Result | DB test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **₹50,000 transfer** Mint Savan Bank → Tijori | txn, txn_entity (Mint), 2 legs, 1 journal, 2 lines, 2 slices updated, audit | ≥ 2 lines with Dr and Cr per fund (deferred); account and fund belong to Mint (composite FKs); location required on money lines; period open | Dr = Cr; positive whole rupees; source ≠ destination | Mint's total and fund unchanged; Tijori +₹50,000, Savan −₹50,000 | `ledger_structure_test` |
| 2 | **Insufficient balance**: JSK ₹4,00,000 available, transfer ₹4,50,000 | nothing | — | Slice locked, decrypted, available checked after the lock; refused before any insert | "The available balance is insufficient." No row written | `posting_refusal_test` (API) |
| 3 | **Reserved funds**: JSK ₹5,00,000, ₹1,00,000 reserved | balance_hold (reservation) | Hold belongs to JSK's fund (composite FK); status constrained | available = current − active holds, under lock | Same refusal as #2 at ₹4,50,000; ₹3,50,000 passes | `holds_test` |
| 4 | **Firm-to-firm transfer** JSK → Mint ₹50,000 (inter-entity) | 2 journals (JSK, Mint), open item Mint owes JSK | Each journal's lines in its own books; open item debtor ≠ creditor | Classification required (AC3); reciprocity | JSK receivable = Mint payable = ₹50,000 | `ledger_structure_test` |
| 5 | **Personal expense** Krish pays his own ₹2,000 food | 1 journal [Krish] | RLS: only Krish (and grantees) can read it | — | Invisible to Super Admin and Mint users | `rls_privacy_test` |

| 7 | **Common expense** across Mint and JSK, exact amounts | 1 txn, n allocation legs, one journal per owner, open items | legs frozen after submission | Σ allocations = total, else refused; no automatic split | each owner's expense exact | engine tests + `txn_lifecycle_test` |
| 8 | **₹45,000 multi-entity**: Mint 30,000 + JSK 5,000 + Personal 10,000, paid by Krish | 1 txn, 1 source + 3 allocation legs, 3 journals, 2 open items | personal leg and journal readable only by Krish; lines linked to their legs | Mint owes Krish ₹30,000, JSK owes Krish ₹5,000 | A Mint user sees Mint's ₹30,000 and the debt to Krish, never the ₹10,000 | `rls_privacy_test` |
| 9 | **Firm money → another firm's expense**: Mint pays Firm B's ₹50,000 | journals [Mint], [Firm B]; open item Firm B owes Mint | payer and owner stored separately (legs) | payer records no expense | Firm B expense; Mint receivable | engine tests |

| 12 | **Handover with confirmation ON** | journal to cash-in-transit, custody_event `awaiting_confirmation`; on confirm a second journal | custody status constrained; confirmer recorded | only the receiver can confirm | money in transit until confirmed; dispute → exception | `custody_test` |
| 13 | **Custody change of a place**: Father gives Tijori key to Krish | location_holder: Father row ended, Krish row opened | exactly one current holder (partial unique); history kept | — | access list unchanged (RULEBOOK-03 §13) | `location_facts_test` |
| 14 | **Location access change**: Add C; Replace Krish by C | location_access rows; Replace = one revoke + one grant with one `change_id` | one open row per (location, person) | — | Add: Krish + Father + C; Replace: Father + C | `location_facts_test` |
| 15 | **Unassigned location** (cash in hand, nobody) | location_holder with null person | allowed by schema | — | shown as unassigned | `location_facts_test` |
| 16 | **Reimbursement**: Mint settles ₹30,000 to Krish | settlement txn, journals [Mint], [Krish], allocation | allocation linked to the open item and the settling txn | allocation ≤ remaining | item settled; original expense untouched; no second expense | `settlement_test` |
| 17 | **Advance** ₹50,000 → spent ₹42,000 → ₹8,000 returned | advance open item; advance_account txns; allocations of kind advance_use / advance_return | allocations immutable | remaining 0 ≤ r ≤ original | advance closed; never Sujal's net worth | engine tests + `settlement_test` |
| 18 | **One payment settles several items; one item settled by several payments** | allocations many-to-many | junction with FKs both ways | Σ per item ≤ remaining | correct remainders | engine tests |
| 19 | **Reversal** of a posted transfer | mirror journal (`reversal_of_journal_id`), txn_link `reverses`, status `reversed` | original rows cannot be updated or deleted; a journal reversed at most once; a txn reversed at most once | exact mirror | both visible; balances back | `immutability_test` |
| 20 | **Correction**: ₹5,000 should have been ₹5,500 | reversal + replacement txn, links `reverses` and `corrects` | as #19 | — | full sequence visible (H15) | `immutability_test` |
| 21 | **Reconciliation difference** Tijori system ₹4,82,000, counted ₹4,80,000 | reconciliation row (expected, actual, difference encrypted); after approval an adjustment txn linked | reconciliation never edits a balance (no update path to snapshots outside posting) | adjustment only with approval | Cash over/short ₹2,000 linked to the reconciliation | `reconciliation_test` |
